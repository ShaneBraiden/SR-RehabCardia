Cardiac rehabilitation, guided by your care team

RehabCardia connects patients recovering from cardiac events with their doctors — personalised exercise programs, daily progress tracking, and in-app messaging.

Available for Android. Accounts are provided by your clinic. Powered by SRET-AIDA.

What the app does

Doctor-assigned exercises

Video-guided sessions prescribed by your doctor, with sets, reps, and rest periods built in.

Progress tracking

Daily progress rings and history charts show your adherence and recovery over time.

Messaging

Chat with your doctor and share post-workout feedback — pain, difficulty, and notes.

Appointments & reminders

Scheduled appointments and exercise reminders keep your program on track.

How it works

  1. Your clinic sets you up Your doctor or clinic administrator creates your account and builds a rehabilitation plan for your condition.
  2. Follow your program Sign in, complete video-guided sessions at your own pace, and log how each workout felt.
  3. Your doctor stays in the loop Your care team reviews your progress and feedback, adjusts your plan, and messages you when it matters.

Your data, protected

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest — all communication uses HTTPS/TLS, and stored data is encrypted on our servers and on your device.
  • Role-based access — patients see only their own records; doctors see only the patients under their care.
  • Never sold, no ads — your data is used only to deliver your care program.
Medical notice: RehabCardia supports — but does not replace — the care you receive from your healthcare provider. If you experience chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or dizziness, stop exercising and seek immediate medical attention.
