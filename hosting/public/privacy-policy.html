Privacy Policy — RehabCardia

Effective date: 12 June 2026 · Last updated: 12 June 2026

RehabCardia ("the App", "we", "us") is a digital cardiac rehabilitation platform operated by SRET-AIDA. It connects patients recovering from cardiac events with their treating doctors, who assign exercises, monitor progress, and communicate through the App. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we collect only what is needed to deliver your rehabilitation program, we never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.

1. Who creates your account

RehabCardia accounts are created by your healthcare provider (your doctor or the clinic administrator). You cannot self-register. By using the credentials given to you, you consent to the processing described in this policy as part of your care program.

2. Information we collect

Information your care team provides: first name, last name, email address; your assigned doctor, rehabilitation plan, and prescribed exercises (sets, repetitions, schedule, duration).

Information generated as you use the App: exercise and session completion data; post-workout feedback such as pain level, difficulty rating, and optional notes; chat messages with your care team; appointments and confirmations; login timestamps used for account security and clinical audit; and a Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to deliver reminders and notifications.

Information collected automatically: basic technical information needed for the App to function (authentication state, app version). We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos (except media you explicitly attach), or browsing history.

3. How we use your information

  • Deliver your prescribed rehabilitation program and track your progress
  • Let your doctor review your adherence, feedback, and recovery trends
  • Send appointment reminders, exercise reminders, and care-team messages (push notifications are optional and controlled by your device settings)
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service, including login auditing
  • Comply with legal obligations applicable to health services

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling unrelated to your care, or sale to third parties.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Storage), operated by Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted by role-based security rules: patients can only access their own records; doctors can access records of patients under their care; administrators access data solely for clinical and operational management.

Exercise demonstration videos may be hosted as unlisted videos on YouTube. These videos are instructional content produced by the care team and do not contain patient data.

5. Who can see your data

  • Your care team: your assigned doctor and clinic administrators
  • You: your own records, through the App
  • Service providers: Google (Firebase) as our data processor

We do not share your personal data with any other third party unless required by law.

6. Data retention

Your records are retained for as long as your account is active, as part of your clinical care record. If your account is deleted by your clinic, your profile and associated care data (plans, assignments, sessions, messages, notifications) are deleted from our active systems. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged on the backup rotation cycle.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because your account is managed by your healthcare provider, please direct requests to your clinic, or contact us directly at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

To request account deletion, contact your clinic or email us at the address below. Deletion of clinical records may be subject to medical record retention laws applicable to your provider.

8. Children

RehabCardia is intended for adult cardiac rehabilitation patients and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), encrypted storage, role-based access rules enforced on the server, encrypted on-device preference storage, and Play Integrity–backed app verification. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data with appropriate technical and organisational measures.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by your clinic. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
