Delete Your RehabCardia Account

Last updated: 15 July 2026

RehabCardia accounts are created and managed by your healthcare provider, doctor, or clinic administrator. You can request deletion of your account and associated app data using the steps below.

Quick request: email sr.cardiocare@gmail.com with the subject "RehabCardia account deletion request".

How to request account deletion

  1. Contact your clinic, doctor, or clinic administrator and ask them to delete your RehabCardia account.
  2. Alternatively, email sr.cardiocare@gmail.com from the email address used for your RehabCardia account.
  3. Include your full name, account email address, and clinic or doctor name so we can identify the correct account.
  4. We will verify the request and respond within 30 days.

Data deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete your RehabCardia profile and associated active app data, including:

  • name, email address, phone number, and profile details
  • assigned rehabilitation plans and exercise assignments
  • workout/session completion history
  • post-workout feedback, pain level, difficulty rating, and notes
  • messages, notifications, appointment confirmations, and device push token

Data that may be retained

Some records may be retained when required for medical record, legal, security, audit, or backup obligations. This may include limited clinical records retained by your healthcare provider and security logs needed to protect the service.

Residual copies in encrypted backups are removed on the normal backup rotation cycle. Deletion of clinical records may be subject to medical record retention laws applicable to your provider.
